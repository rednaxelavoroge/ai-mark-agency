## 2. From idea to a working business

**Business Creation** is one of the company's core models.

A person or a company can come to us:

- with a ready business idea
- with capital, but without a specific idea
- with an existing business that needs to be scaled
- with a technology or expertise that needs to become a commercial product

If an idea already exists, we analyse it:

**idea → market → competitors → audience → economics → business model → launch strategy**

If the initial idea does not look optimal, we can propose changing it or consider alternative models.

If there is no specific idea, the company can conduct market research and formulate **several potential business concepts** based on available capital, interests, resources and market opportunities.

The client receives not just a consultation, but a worked-out version of **which business can be created and how to launch it**.

Once a concept is selected, execution begins:

**Business Concept → Brand → Product → Website / Platform → AI → Marketing → Sales → Launch → Growth**
